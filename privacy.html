Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 12, 2026 • Architecture: Client-First Transformation

Summary of Key Principles

1. We do not require accounts, logins, or personal registration.

2. Whenever possible, your documents are processed locally inside your browser and never leave your computer.

3. Files processed on our server are held in transient memory only and permanently deleted immediately after conversion.

4. We do not sell, rent, or monetize your documents or personal data.

1. Information We Collect

File Content: We process the document, spreadsheet, presentation, image, or ZIP archive you select strictly to execute the format transformation requested.

Technical Logs: Standard web hosting infrastructure automatically registers non-identifying server telemetry including IP address, browser user-agent, request timestamp, and HTTP status codes to prevent abuse and ensure service availability.

Support Communications: If you submit a report via our Contact page, we collect your name, email address, issue category, and message text solely to respond to your technical inquiry.

2. Dual-Engine File Processing Architecture

Client-Side Local Processing: Many of our conversion tools (including PDF to Word, Word to PDF, Word to PowerPoint, PowerPoint to Word, ZIP Files, UNZIP Archive, Merge PDF, Split PDF, Remove Pages, and Watermark) prioritize in-browser execution using client-side JavaScript. In this mode, document bytes never leave your device.

Server-Side Compilation Fallback: If a document requires specialized server libraries (such as LibreOffice Writer, Poppler, or QPDF), your file is transmitted over encrypted TLS 1.3 to our backend. The file is temporarily stored in an isolated operating system directory, converted, streamed back to you, and immediately unlinked from disk using automated cleanup routines.

3. Artificial Intelligence Features

When you use the AI Summarizer or Translate Document tools, the text or page images extracted from your document are sent via an encrypted TLS connection to the Google Gemini API. This data is transmitted solely to synthesize the summary or produce the translation and is governed by Google’s enterprise API data policies. Document text is not used to train machine learning models.

4. Cookies and Local Storage

Fileora does not use tracking or advertising cookies. We use browser localStorage solely to save your light/dark theme preference (localStorage.theme).

5. Data Retention & Zero Storage Policy

We do not maintain user databases, document archives, or conversion history logs. Once your download finishes, or when you close your browser tab, your document is no longer retained in our active system memory.

6. Your Rights & Contact

Because we do not store personal profiles or document accounts, we hold no persistent personal data linked to your identity. For any privacy inquiries, reach our engineering team via our Contact Support Page.
